About The Project

Understanding PET Imaging, Low-Dose Challenges, and the Power of Deep Learning AI in Healthcare.

What is PET Imaging?

Positron Emission Tomography (PET) is a non-invasive nuclear medicine functional imaging technique that visualizes cellular metabolic processes in the human body.

Radiotracers (such as Fluorodeoxyglucose - 18F-FDG) are injected into the patient. As the radiotracer decays, it emits positrons that annihilate with electrons, emitting pairs of 511 keV gamma photons detected by the PET scanner ring to construct 3D metabolic maps.

  • Oncology: Detecting tumor metastases and tracking chemotherapy response.
  • Neurology: Early diagnosis of Alzheimer's disease and epileptic foci.
  • Cardiology: Assessing myocardial viability post-infarction.

Low-Dose vs. High-Dose PET

Low-Dose PET

Reduced radiotracer injection (1/4 to 1/10th dose). Low radiation risk, but high Poisson noise and poor signal-to-noise ratio (SNR).

High-Dose PET

Standard radiotracer dose. Crisp metabolic detail, clear lesion boundaries, but higher cumulative radiation dose to patient organs.

Scanning patients at full dose poses long-term stochastic radiation risk (ALARA principle: As Low As Reasonably Achievable). Low-Dose scanning is safer, but raw low-dose images are severely degraded by noise, making clinical diagnosis difficult.

Why Image Enhancement is Needed

Image enhancement bridges the gap between patient safety and diagnostic accuracy. Conventional filtering methods (Gaussian smoothing, BM3D) cause spatial blur and lose sharp lesion boundaries.

Key Clinical Objectives:
  • Denoise Poisson-dominated photon counting noise without blurring anatomical edges.
  • Restore Standardized Uptake Value (SUV) quantitative fidelity.
  • Enable ultra-low dose PET imaging protocols in pediatric care.

Advantages of AI in Medical Imaging

Deep Learning architectures like U-Net learn complex non-linear spatial mapping directly from paired Low-Dose and High-Dose PET datasets.

  • Sub-Second Inference: Processes 2D DICOM slices in milliseconds compared to computationally expensive iterative reconstruction methods.
  • Feature-Aware Denoising: Skip connections preserve fine structural background while suppressing noise.
  • End-to-End Consistency: Direct image-to-image translation optimized on L1 structural loss.
