Clinical Challenge

Project Overview

PET scanning delivers essential metabolic diagnostics — but full-dose protocols expose patients to significant ionizing radiation. This system closes that gap with deep learning.

Radiation Reduction

Low-dose PET protocols cut radiotracer activity, lowering risk for pediatric and recurring-scan patients without abandoning diagnostic PET.

U-Net Deep Learning

Encoder–decoder architecture with skip connections, trained on paired low-dose / full-dose slices to restore metabolic detail.

Quantitative Quality

High PSNR and SSIM restoration (~93% mean SSIM) without introducing phantom metabolic artifacts.
